## Goal

Participate smartly in case there's a potential airdrop in the future (nothing is confirmed yet).

## 1. Community (Arc House) – Priority #1

This is the most important part right now. Points help you level up in the Architects program. The more active you are, the better.

| Step | What to do | How to do it | Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Sign up for Arc House** | Go to <a href="https://community.arc.network" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">community.arc.network</a> → Sign up using Google or email | Once |
| **2** | **Complete your profile** | Click your avatar → Edit Profile and fill in all your details | Once |
| **3** | **Earn daily points (easy)** | Log in every day (+1 point) | Daily |
| **4** | **Read content** | Go to the Content section and read articles (2 points per article, max 5 a day) | Daily |
| **5** | **Watch videos** | Look for articles with videos and watch them (5 points per video, max 4 a day) | Daily |
| **6** | **Arc Architect** | Once you reach 500 points you can request the Architect badge on your profile | Whenever possible |
| **7** | **Engage more (recommended)** | Join the Discord (<a href="https://discord.gg/buildonarc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">discord.gg/buildonarc</a>), attend Office Hours and events | Whenever possible |

Becoming an **Architect** user will allow you to share ideas, be part of the builder and contributor team in the community, earn many more points, and subsequently join their official Discord server.

## 2. Testnet (After community engagement)

Once you're already active in the community, proceed with these actions on the test network.

### Add the Arc Testnet

Set up your wallet (like MetaMask or Rabby) with the following details:
<div class="flex flex-wrap gap-2 my-4 not-prose">
  <ResourceCard id="metamask" label="Download Metamask" /> <ResourceCard id="rabby" label="Download Rabby" />
</div>

- **Network Name:** Arc Testnet
- **RPC:** `https://rpc.testnet.arc.network`
- **Chain ID:** `5042002`
- **Symbol:** USDC
- **Explorer:** <a href="https://testnet.arcscan.app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">testnet.arcscan.app</a>

Or you can visit: <ResourceCard id="chainlist" label="Chainlist" /> and search for **Arc Testnet**, once found, click on **Add to Metamask**.

<div class="flex flex-wrap gap-2 my-4 not-prose">

</div>

### Get testnet tokens

1. Go to <a href="https://faucet.circle.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">faucet.circle.com</a>
2. Select **Arc Testnet**
3. Request USDC, EURC and/or cirBTC (you can request every 2 hours)

### Interact on the Testnet

- Send USDC to another wallet
- Deploy a simple contract
- Use dApps running on the Arc Testnet like <a href="https://app.synthra.org/#/?chain=arc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">Synthra</a>. Make a swap, provide liquidity, and use their perpetuals trading interface.
- **Explore:** Use the <a href="https://testnet.arcscan.app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">Arcscan</a> explorer to view your transactions.
