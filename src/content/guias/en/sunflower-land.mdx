## Step 3: Introduction to basic mechanics

In Sunflower Land, your initial farm is located on a small island that you can gradually expand. To unlock new land and grow, you must master the basic game loop.

      <h3 class="text-base font-bold text-white mb-2">1. Island Management</h3>
      <p class="text-sm text-terminal-secondary leading-relaxed">Your island is divided into expansions. To unlock new spaces, you will need to spend resources (food, wood, minerals) and level up. As you expand your land, you will discover more resource nodes.</p>
