## Introduction to Season 16: Cave Dwellers

Seasons in Sunflower Land are 90-day quarterly chapters centered around specific thematic updates and interconnected mechanics. **Season 16 (Chapter 16: Cave Dwellers)** officially runs from **November 2, 2026** until **February 1, 2027**.

Sunflower Land **does not follow a single linear progression**: every farmer has a different Bumpkin level, distinct personal objectives, and unique resource allocations. While some players dedicate the entire 90 days to clearing out **Stella's Megastore** by collecting chapter tickets, others master the **pig husbandry and truffle supply chain**, some pursue **rare seasonal mutants**, and others trade high-demand materials across the **P2P Marketplace**.

Below is a detailed breakdown of each seasonal system: what it does, its required Bumpkin level, how to unlock it, and how to optimize it according to your playstyle.

## 1. Season Pass and Stella's Megastore

The economic centerpiece of any chapter in Sunflower Land revolves around task deliveries and seasonal exchanges at **Stella's Megastore** in the central Plaza.

### The Season Pass (Cave Dwellers Banner)
The **Cave Dwellers Banner** is the official chapter pass available in the Plaza at launch.
* **Core Passive Boost:** Placing the Banner on your farm awards **+1 extra Beetle Wing** on every single delivery (*Deliveries*) and Bounty completed throughout the 90-day period.
* **Additional Perks:** Grants exclusive Megastore item discounts, priority access to seasonal crafting recipes, and complimentary seasonal raffle tickets.

### Seasonal Currency: Beetle Wings
The official currency for this chapter is the **Beetle Wing (Token ID #3103)**.
* **How to Obtain:**
  1. **NPC Deliveries:** Fulfilling orders for crops, cooked kitchen dishes, and fish requested by Plaza characters.
  2. **Bounties Board:** Completing weekly resource gathering and specialized delivery tasks.
  3. **Chore List Quests:** Rotating daily and weekly farm chores.
* **Purpose:** Non-transferable seasonal vouchers spent at **Stella's Megastore** for community monuments, wearables, decorations, and raffle tickets. **They expire when the chapter concludes**, so ensure they are spent before February 1, 2027.

### Chapter Raffle Tickets (Cave Dwellers Raffle Ticket - ID #3104)
Players can convert excess Beetle Wings into chapter raffle tickets to enter periodic seasonal drawings featuring ultra-rare NFTs, high-value SFTs, and exclusive wearables.

## 2. Underground Caves and the Myco-Composter (Requirement: Level 11+)

The subterranean caves introduce underground organic farming and excavation on **Spring Island**.

### Unlock Requirements
* **Bumpkin Level Required:** **Level 11**.
* **Initial Entrance Cost (Tier I):** 200 Coins, 20 Wood, and 5 Stone (takes 1 hour to construct).
* **Initial Chamber:** Unlocks the first underground room equipped with 1 processing machine (*Myco-Composter*) and a dedicated 25-tile (5x5) digging grid.

### Myco-Composter Recipes (Base Duration: 12 Hours)
The Myco-Composter processes surface resources into harvestable subterranean patches:
* **Mushrooms:** Input: 10 Wood. Generates Wild Mushroom tiles. Each tile has a **10% chance** to become a Magic Mushroom. *(Note: The introductory test batch takes only 10 minutes).*
* **Beetles:** Input: 10 Eggs. Generates tiles containing beetles across 4 rarity tiers.
* **Mud:** Input: 10 Crops (Carrot equivalents). Generates Mud tiles essential for pigpen hygiene and maintenance.

### No Daily Dig Cap
Unlike Desert Digging, subterranean cave patches feature **NO daily digging limit**. You may excavate as many tiles as your available stock of **Sand Shovels** and **Sand Drills** permits.

### The 4 Beetle Tiers: Direct Animal Feed & Market
Beetles can be traded on the P2P marketplace or fed directly to **Pigs, Cows, Sheep, or Chickens** for an immediate XP boost:
* **Brown Beetle:** Grants **80 XP** direct animal feed.
* **Blue Beetle:** Grants **160 XP** direct animal feed.
* **Pink Beetle:** Grants **320 XP** direct animal feed.
* **Amber Beetle:** Grants **640 XP** direct animal feed.

### Cave Expansions (Tier I to VIII)
Caves expand across 8 tiers, accommodating up to 8 Myco-Composters and 200 concurrent digging tiles. Starting at **Tier V onward**, expansions replace leather requirements with **Pig Bristles** and accumulated Mud.

## 3. Pig Husbandry: Pigs, Pigpen, and Truffles (Requirement: Level 30+)

Pigs introduce a high-tier animal husbandry cycle focused on truffles, bristle retirement, and mud hygiene.

### Pigpen Unlock & Capacity Rules
* **Bumpkin Level Required:** **Level 30**.
* **Housing Capacity:** 3, 6, or 9 pigs depending on Pigpen upgrade level.
* **Purchase Cost:** The first **3 starting pigs are free**; subsequent pigs cost 100 Coins each.
* **Max Level:** Pigs progress from **Level 1 to Level 15**, mirroring cow XP progression curves.

### Crop Weight Feeding Formula
Pigs accept all eligible crops. The required feed weight scales according to the formula:
`Weight = √(Seed Cost in Coins × Base Grow Time in Minutes)`
* **Anchor Benchmarks:** 10 Carrots / 3 Cauliflower / 1 Wheat.

### Mud Upkeep: Sickness Immunity & +25% Final XP
Mud harvested from the Myco-Composter is crucial for swine health:
* Applying **10 Mud** to a pig grants **3 bathing charges**.
* Each charge yields a **+25% bonus to final feeding XP** (including direct beetle feedings).
* As long as a pig holds active mud charges, it is **completely immune to sickness**.

### Truffle Production
Truffles are collected automatically upon completing each 24-hour sleep cycle:
* **Levels 1 to 3:** 1 Truffle per cycle (~3 cumulative truffles).
* **Levels 4 to 7:** 2 Trufas per cycle (~8 cumulative truffles).
* **Levels 8 to 14:** 3 Trufas per cycle (~21 cumulative truffles).
* **Level 15:** 4 Trufas per cycle continuously (4 truffles/day per pig).

### Pig Bristles: Retirement Trade-In
**Pig Bristles** are NOT awarded during daily sleep. They are earned exclusively when **retiring / trading in a pig at the Pigpen** or completing designated Bounties:
* **Retire at Level 3:** 1 Pig Bristle.
* **Retire at Level 6:** 3 Pig Bristles.
* **Retire at Level 10:** 6 Pig Bristles.
* **Retire at Level 15:** 12 Pig Bristles.
* **Sickness Penalty:** Retiring a sick pig incurs a **-25% penalty** on awarded bristles.
* **Utility:** Bristles are essential for high-tier Cave expansions (Tier V–VIII) and weather protection crafting.

## 4. Truffle Cooking, Truffle Seasoning, and Goblin Cave Merchant

Truffles enable major culinary XP leaps and high-efficiency animal acceleration:

### New Truffle Kitchen Recipes
* **Ember-Roasted Truffle Potatoes (Fire Pit):** 8 hours | Truffles + Potatoes | **15,000 XP**.
* **Truffle & Honey Cake (Bakery):** 12 hours | 15 Truffles + 30 Wheat + 30 Eggs + 5 Honey | **25,000 XP**.
* **Truffle Risotto (Kitchen):** 16 hours | Truffles + Rice / High-tier crops | **35,000 XP**.

### Truffle Seasoning (Animal Acceleration)
Crafted at the Spice Rack / Aging Shed in batches of 5 units per 1 hour of processing:
* **Operational Effect:** Reduces wake and sleep cycle durations by **-50% across ALL farm animals** (Pigs, Cows, Sheep, and Chickens).
* **Duration:** Lasts 1 complete sleep cycle (or 2 cycles when holding the *Vibraphone* collectible). Highly prized by competitive livestock managers.

### Goblin Cave Merchant
* Appears inside the cave for **48-hour scheduled visits**.
* Offers rotating seasonal items at discounted bulk rates.
* Starts with 1 offer at Cave Tier I, gaining **+1 concurrent offer per additional cave tier unlocked**.

## 5. Dynamic Global Weather System (9 Events)

Season 16 implements a continuous worldwide weather rotation affecting all farmers simultaneously:
* **9 Global Weather Events:** Each event lasts **3 to 4 days** (~27 to 36 days per full cycle).
* **Forecast:** An in-game meter displays upcoming conditions **72 hours in advance**.
* **Normal vs "Blessed" Status:** Players who equip mitigation gear not only avoid penalties, but unlock high-tier blessed multipliers.

| Weather Event | Normal Effect | "Blessed" Effect (Equipped with Mitigation) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bountiful Harvest** | +0.4 crop yield | **+0.8 crop yield** |
| **Fish Frenzy** | +0.4 fish per catch | **+0.8 fish** per catch |
| **Sun Shower** | 1.3x base crop growth speed | **1.6x to 2.0x growth speed** (with Shade Cloth & Guardian) |
| **Heavy Rains** | Moderate well acceleration | Rapid well water recovery |
| **Bee Migration** | Base honey boost | **+187.5% to +225% honey** with active Wildflower Lures |

### Weather Protection Recipes
* **Thermal Stone, Shade Cloth, and Merino Wool Insulation:** Each requires **25 Pig Bristles + 10 Merino Wool**.
* **Sandbag and Balk:** Each requires **2 Leather**.
* **Wildflower Lure:** Consumes 1 Flower (lasts 1 to 5 days based on flower tier) and maximizes honey output during favorable events.

## 7. Strategies by Player Profile: What to Focus On?

Because a 90-day season features multiple simultaneous paths, focus on the route that matches your Bumpkin level and personal goals:

### Route A: Stella's Megastore Collector (Pass & Deliveries)
* **Requirement:** Any Bumpkin level.
* **Goal:** Collect the maximum number of **Beetle Wings** before the store closes on February 1st.
* **Strategy:** Purchase the *Cave Dwellers Banner* on day one for the `+1 Beetle Wing` bonus on all orders. Direct crop and fish production toward fulfilling NPC Deliveries and Bounty tasks. Reserve wings for high-priority Megastore items (*Duende Monument* and *Miner's Bed*).

### Route B: Swineherd & Truffle Master (Level 30+)
* **Requirement:** Bumpkin Level 30.
* **Goal:** Maximize Truffle yields, power-level Bumpkin XP via gourmet recipes, and accelerate animal breeding.
* **Strategy:** Claim your 3 free starting pigs and feed them Carrot, Cauliflower, or Wheat batches. Keep the mud bath (10 Mud) permanently active for the +25% XP multiplier and sickness immunity. Craft **Truffle Seasoning** continuously to halve animal sleep times. Retire pigs at Level 6 or 10 when you need **Pig Bristles** for weather protections and high-tier cave upgrades.

### Route C: Subterranean Prospector (Level 11+)
* **Requirement:** Bumpkin Level 11.
* **Goal:** Leverage the zero daily dig cap to farm Wild Mushrooms, Magic Mushrooms, and Beetles.
* **Strategy:** Construct the Cave Entrance on Spring Island. Feed wood into mushroom batches and eggs into beetle batches. Use beetles for rapid animal XP boosts or sell them on the P2P marketplace. Stockpile Sand Shovels and Sand Drills to clear 25-tile patches as soon as Myco-Composters finish.

### Route D: Mutant Collector
* **Requirement:** Active livestock pens and flower gardens.
* **Goal:** Acquire all 4 seasonal mutants (*Mushroom Chicken*, *Ladybug Cow*, *Beetle Sheep*, *Cyclops Bloom*).
* **Strategy:** Maintain consistent breeding cycles and flower cross-pollination. Mutants serve as rare assets with permanent cosmetic value and secondary market demand.

### Route E: P2P Market Arbitrageur / Trader
* **Requirement:** Marketplace access.
* **Goal:** Earn FLOWER and Coins by trading high-volatility seasonal commodities.
* **Strategy:** Demand for **Merino Wool**, **Leather**, **Truffles**, **Mud**, and **Beetles** spikes around weather events and cave expansions. Monitor real-time prices using 0x Leñador tools and sell surpluses to players racing to upgrade.
