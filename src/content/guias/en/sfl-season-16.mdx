## Introduction to Season 16

**Season 16 (Chapter 16: Caves, Pigs & Weather)** officially begins on **November 2, 2026** in Sunflower Land. This major update shifts gameplay around three core interconnected economic pillars:

1. **The Caves:** Unlocked on Spring Island, featuring the new **Myco-Composter** processing machines, 25-tile excavation patches producing Wild Mushrooms, Magic Mushrooms, 4 tiers of Beetles, and Mud.
2. **Pig Husbandry (Pigs & Pigpen):** Level 1 to 15 Pigs, recurring **Truffles** harvests, **Pig Bristles** trade-ins, and **Mud** health/XP management (+25% final XP).
3. **Dynamic Global Weather System:** Non-stop rotation of 9 worldwide weather events (lasting 3 to 4 days each, completing cycles every ~27 to 36 days) with positive bonuses and seasonal threats mitigated through craftable protection gear.
4. **Goblin Cave Merchant:** Visiting vendor offering exclusive items for 48-hour periods, unlocked starting at Cave Tier I.

## Step 2: The Underground Caves and the Myco-Composter

The Cave Entrance unlocks on **Spring Island**. Building the entrance gives access to the first subterranean chamber with one **Myco-Composter** machine and a dedicated 25-tile (5x5) digging grid.

### Cave Access & Expansion Tiers

* **Entrance Cost (Tier I):** 200 Coins, 20 Wood, and 5 Stone.
* **Expansion Path:** Up to 8 levels (Tier VIII). Each expansion adds 1 Myco-Composter and an independent 25-tile patch (reaching 8 machines and 200 tiles total).
* **High-Tier Materials:** From **Tier V onward**, upgrades require **Pig Bristles** instead of Leather, along with accumulated Mud.

### Myco-Composter Batches (Base: 12 Hours)

Each machine runs any of the three available recipes. Standard batch time is 12 hours:

| Recipe | Required Input | 25-Tile Excavation Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mushrooms** | 10 Wood | Wild Mushroom tiles. Each tile has a **10% chance** to yield a Magic Mushroom. |
| **Beetles** | 10 Eggs | Beetle tiles across 4 rarity tiers (Brown, Blue, Pink, Amber). |
| **Mud** | 10 Crops (Carrot equivalents) | Mud tiles used for pig baths and health maintenance. |

### Excavation Tools

* Digging uses standard **Sand Shovels** and **Sand Drills**.
* **Key difference from Desert:** Caves have **NO daily dig cap**. Dig as many patches as your tool supply allows.
* Cave patches do not contain prior chapter artifacts.
* Cave Skill Tier 2 boosts purchasable stock of Shovels & Drills in the shop by **+50% / +100% / +200%**.

### Beetles: Rarity & Direct Feeding

All 4 beetle types are tradable on the P2P market and available from Cave Tier I:

* **Brown Beetle:** **80 XP** direct animal feed.
* **Blue Beetle:** **160 XP** direct animal feed.
* **Pink Beetle:** **320 XP** direct animal feed.
* **Amber Beetle:** **640 XP** direct animal feed.

*Direct feeding note:* Beetles can be fed directly to **Pigs, Cows, Sheep, or Chickens**.

## Step 3: Pig Husbandry, Truffles, and Pig Bristles

Pigs introduce a full lifecycle with level-up milestones, nutrition preferences, and dual resource yields.

### Pigpen Capacity & Rules

* **Housing Capacity:** 3 / 6 / 9 pigs based on Pigpen tier.
* **Purchase Cost:** 100 Coins per pig (first **3 starting pigs are free**).
* **Max Level:** Any pig can reach **Level 15** from Pigpen Level 1, following Cow XP progression curves.
* **Target Farm Size:** Midrange farms aim to maintain 6 to 9 synchronized pigs through levels 0 to 8. Higher levels require significant feed stockpiling.

### Pig Feeding

Pigs accept all eligible crops at any level with preferred-band efficiency. Crop weight follows the formula: `Weight = √(Seed Coin Cost × Base Grow Time in Minutes)`.

* **Tier Anchors:** 10 Carrots / 3 Cauliflower / 1 Wheat.
* Regular fish classify according to their standard fishing categories.

### Mud Upkeep: Protection & +25% Final XP

* Applying **10 Mud** to a pig grants **3 feeding charges**.
* Each charge yields **+25% final feeding XP** (including direct Beetle feedings).
* Charges persist across sleep cycles.
* While charges remain, pigs are **immune to sickness** (does not cure an already sick pig).

### Truffle Production

Truffles are collected every 24-hour sleep cycle starting at Level 1 and are fully tradable:

| Pig Level | Truffles per Claim | Cumulative Truffles Produced to Level 15 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Levels 1 – 3** | 1 Truffle / cycle | ~3 Truffles |
| **Levels 4 – 7** | 2 Truffles / cycle | ~8 Truffles |
| **Levels 8 – 14** | 3 Truffles / cycle | ~21 Truffles |
| **Level 15** | 4 Truffles / cycle (daily) | 4 Truffles / day continuously |

A farm with 6 mature pigs generates **24 Truffles per day** before extra bonuses.

### Pig Bristles

Bristles are **NOT collected through daily sleep**; they are awarded solely upon **trading-in / retiring** pigs at the Pigpen or completing Bounty tasks:

| Retirement Milestone | Base Bristles Awarded |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Level 3** | 1 Bristle |
| **Level 6** | 3 Bristles |
| **Level 10** | 6 Bristles |
| **Level 15** | 12 Bristles |

* **Sickness Penalty:** Sick pigs suffer a **-25% penalty** on awarded Bristles.
* Bristles are tradable and essential for high-tier Cave expansions (Tier V–VIII) and Weather protection crafts.

## Step 4: Dynamic Global Weather and Hazard Mitigation

Season 16 features **one active global weather event at all times**:

* **Continuous Cycle:** 9 global weather events, lasting **3 to 4 days** each (~27 to 36 days total cycle).
* **Weather Forecast:** Upcoming events are previewed **72 hours in advance**.
* **Overlays:** Full Moon and Double Delivery continue as separate 24-hour overlays.

### Weather Events & Magnitudes

| Weather Event | Normal Magnitude | Blessed Magnitude |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bountiful Harvest** | +0.4 crop yield | +0.8 crop yield |
| **Fish Frenzy** | +0.4 fish yield | +0.8 fish yield |
| **Sun Shower** | 1.3x crop speed | 1.6x with Shade Cloth / Guardian (2.0x with both) |
| **Heavy Rains** | Well speed & water recovery | Enhanced water bonuses |
| **Bee Migration** | Honey production bonus | Substantial honey boost with Wildflower Lures |

### Weather Protection Recipes

* **Thermal Stone, Merino Wool Insulation, Shade Cloth:** Each requires **25 Pig Bristles + 10 Merino Wool**.
* **Sandbag & Balk:** Each requires **2 Leather**.
* **Wildflower Lure:** Consumes **1 Flower** (lasts 1 to 5 days based on flower tier). Adds **+125% to +150% Honey** (+187.5% to +225% during Blessed conditions).

## Step 5: Advanced Cooking, Truffle Seasoning, and Cave Merchant

Truffles unlock high-XP gourmet kitchen meals and animal cycle acceleration treats:

### New Cooking Recipes

| Recipe | Cook Location | Duration | Key Requirements | XP Awarded |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ember-Roasted Truffle Potatoes** | Fire Pit | 8 hours | Truffles + Potatoes | 15,000 XP |
| **Truffle & Honey Cake** | Bakery | 12 hours | 15 Truffles + 30 Wheat + 30 Eggs + 5 Honey | 25,000 XP |
| **Truffle Risotto** | Kitchen | 16 hours | Truffles + Rice / High-tier crops | 35,000 XP |

### Truffle Seasoning (Spice Rack / Aging Shed)

* Crafted in batches of **5 units per 1 hour**.
* **Effect:** Grants **-50% wake/sleep time** across **ALL four animal types** (Pigs, Cows, Sheep, Chickens).
* **Duration:** 1 sleep cycle (2 with *Vibraphone*).

### Goblin Cave Merchant

* Unlocked starting at **Cave Tier I**.
* Features 1 exclusive offer at Tier I, adding **+1 offer per cave tier unlocked**.
* Visits occur for **48 hours** with advance announcements and per-player quotas.
