## Step 1: Get Ready

1. **Install an EVM wallet** (Rabby, MetaMask, etc.)
2. **Add the Soneium Mainnet network:** find the RPC at <a href="https://soneium.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">soneium.org</a> or on <a href="https://chainlist.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">Chainlist.org</a>
3. **Have some ETH or tokens on Soneium** to cover bridging gas
4. **Connect to the portal:** <a href="https://portal.soneium.org/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">portal.soneium.org</a> → **Connect Wallet**
5. **Complete 4 campaigns:** And navigate the interface to track your points and transactions.

## Step 2: Understand how points add up

Points are accumulated through multiple simultaneous activities:

| Activity              | Description                                  |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 🔄 **Transactions**   | Swaps, bridges, and any on-chain interaction |
| 💧 **Liquidity (LP)** | Providing liquidity in ecosystem DeFi pools  |
| 🎨 **NFTs**           | Holding badges and ecosystem collectibles    |
| 🎯 **Quests**         | Specific missions with point bonuses         |
| 📅 **Active Days**    | Daily consistency (streaks)                  |

> 💡 **Key:** Points don't come from a single action but from the combination of all of them. Daily consistency multiplies the result.

## Step 3: Daily farming and quests (the most important part)

### Connect with the Startale App (mandatory home base)

The Startale app is the central hub for maximizing points with the least effort:

1. Go to <a href="https://app.startale.com/sign-up?code=VYANVN66" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">app.startale.com</a>
2. Log in with the same wallet
3. Complete **carnival mini-apps and daily quests** — includes games like:
   - Morning Moon Pocket
   - World of Trinity
   - Burrow Bash
   - (and whatever is added per season)

These simultaneously award **STAR points + score on Soneium**.

### Daily on-chain activities

Combine all of the following to maximize:

- **Daily Swaps:** Use ecosystem DEXs (Uniswap or others on Soneium)
- **Provide liquidity:** Even small amounts ($10–$50) in pools generate points
- **Use ecosystem dApps:** Explore at <a href="https://soneium.org/ecosystem" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">soneium.org/ecosystem</a>

### Mint NFTs and badges

This is one of the most underrated point vectors:

- Look for badges from projects like **Arkada**, **Rubyscore**, **NFTs2Me**, etc.
- Holding OG or ecosystem NFTs gives extra points (up to several points per NFT)
- Many are free or very cheap to mint

### Bonus Quests (check the portal regularly)

- Complete missions on **Arkada.gg**, **WheelX**, etc.
- Daily GM on participating apps
- Invites and referrals when applicable
- Follow current campaigns at <a href="https://x.com/soneium" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">@soneium</a> and <a href="https://x.com/startaleapp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-emerald-500 hover:underline">@StartaleApp</a> on X

## Step 4: Monitor and claim

In the portal you can view in real time:

- Your **current score** and progression
- The **badges earned**
- The **season countdown**

At the end of the season (or when you reach **80+ points**), you can **mint the Season Badge (SBT)**.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Seasons last approximately **28 days**. Be consistent from day 1 to maximize your final score.

## Tips to maximize (hit 100/100)

- ✅ **Active every day** — streaks multiply points
- ✅ **Combine Startale + on-chain activity** — don't choose one, use both
- ✅ **Mint cheap NFTs** that give points, even if they are from new collections
- ✅ **Join the Discord and Twitter** of Soneium to find out about updated quests first
- ✅ **Low cost:** many actions are gasless or very cheap via Startale

> ⚠️ **Always DYOR.** Points are for real activity, not spam. Do not invest more than you can afford to lose. Check current quests in the portal because **they change per season**.
